Areas of Expertise and Specialisms

Integrative, evidence-based therapeutic approaches

🧩

Child and Adolescent Mental Health (CAMHS)

With experience in Tier 4 inpatient units, I offer specialist therapy for adolescents with complex trauma, PTSD, mood disorders, suicidal ideation, eating disorders, autism (ASD), OCD and first-episode psychosis. I work closely with families and multidisciplinary teams.

  • ✓ Trauma-informed care
  • ✓ ACT, CFT, CBT, Schema Therapy
  • ✓ Family-centred interventions
⚖️

Forensic Psychology and Risk Management

Extensive experience in low and medium secure forensic services. I provide psychological formulation for severe mental illness, offending behaviour and psychosis, HCR-20 risk assessment, and individual and group interventions (CBTp, anger management, MBTi-based peer support).

  • ✓ Risk assessment (HCR-20)
  • ✓ Work on offending behaviour
  • ✓ Social reintegration
🧠

Neuropsychology and Rehabilitation

Neuropsychological assessment, psychoeducation, stress management and the development of compensatory strategies for people with acquired brain injury. Support with cognitive functions (memory, attention), emotional regulation and independence in everyday activities.

  • ✓ Neuropsychological testing
  • ✓ Cognitive rehabilitation
  • ✓ Carer training
🌿

Intellectual Disability and Positive Behaviour Support

Psychological assessment, formulation and Positive Behaviour Support (PBS) planning in adult residential services and CAMHS. I use trauma-informed, compassion-focused therapy to build emotional regulation and coping strategies.

  • ✓ PBS plans
  • ✓ Staff training
  • ✓ Emotional regulation
💞

Schema Therapy (Complex Trauma and Personality)

ISST-approved training in progress. I work with early maladaptive schemas and their impact on personality patterns and relational dynamics in adulthood: an in-depth, reparenting-based approach for complex trauma and personality disorders.

💻

Online Therapy (English and Spanish)

Online sessions in English and Spanish through secure platforms. Individual therapy for adults experiencing anxiety, depression, low self-esteem and post-traumatic stress disorder.
