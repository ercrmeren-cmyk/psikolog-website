Professional Profile

Clinical Psychologist with international training and extensive NHS experience. I offer evidence-based therapy to children, adolescents and adults, with a strong background in neuropsychology, cognitive assessment and the management of complex cases. I have worked in adult, forensic, CAMHS and neurorehabilitation settings, across both inpatient and outpatient services. I am committed to compassionate, collaborative, high-quality, person-centred care. I am currently specialising in Schema Therapy to work with complex trauma and personality disorders.
